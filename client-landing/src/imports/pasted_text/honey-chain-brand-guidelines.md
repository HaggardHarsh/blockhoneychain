# 19. The biggest change I'd make to your current visual

Your current reference images are **too product-advertisement-ish**.

They communicate:

> 🍯 “Buy delicious honey!”

Your project needs to communicate:

> 🐝 **“Understand where this honey came from.”**

That's a completely different visual language.

So instead of making the hero a giant honey jar, I'd make the **beekeeper + hive + landscape** the hero.

Then use honey jars as secondary imagery.

That makes the project feel much more authentic and aligned with:

**traceability + rural beekeepers + technology + sustainability.**
